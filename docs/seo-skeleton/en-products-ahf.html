块 1 ｜ Active Harmonic Filter (AHF) Series ｜ 1 个标题 ｜ 82 词

  • H1 Active Harmonic Filter (AHF) Series
Every CHITEK AHF compensates harmonics from the 2nd through the 50th order, and you decide how much of each individual order gets corrected. The compensation current tracks whatever the network is producing at that moment instead of running to a fixed curve — cleaner power, less wasted energy.
No two loads distort the waveform the same way, and a single load keeps changing its harmonic signature as operating conditions shift. The filter reads those changes continuously and adapts its output to follow them.

块 4 ｜ Working Principle of Active Harmonic Filter (AHF) ｜ 1 个标题 ｜ 124 词

  • H2 Working Principle of Active Harmonic Filter (AHF)
Inside every CHITEK AHF, an external CT keeps sampling the load current while the DSP at the core runs an advanced control algorithm. It tracks the demanded current as it shifts, splits the measured load current into active and reactive components with an intelligent FFT, and derives the harmonic content quickly and precisely.
The DSP then feeds a PWM signal to the internal IGBT driver board, switching the IGBTs at 20 kHz. The inverter stage outputs a compensation current in opposite phase to the harmonics — and as it does, the CT reads the output current too and returns it to the DSP as negative feedback. That closed loop drives the next control cycle, which is what keeps the correction accurate and the system stable.

块 5 ｜ FAQs About the Active Harmonic Filter (AHF) Series ｜ 1 个标题 ｜ 189 词

  • H2 FAQs About the Active Harmonic Filter (AHF) Series
Q1: How do the models in the AHF range differ?
The range varies on two axes — switching technology (IGBT or SiC MOSFET) and mechanical format (ultra-slim wall mount, distributed, anti-pollution, IP65 outdoor and fanless low-noise). Every model applies the same real-time compensation principle; they differ in switching efficiency, footprint, enclosure rating and acoustic noise.
Q2: Which harmonic orders does the AHF series correct?
Every model in the range compensates harmonic orders from the 2nd through the 50th, and you choose how much of each individual order is corrected. Instead of running to a fixed curve, the compensation current tracks whatever the network is producing at that moment.
Q3: Can AHF modules be combined to reach a higher capacity?
Yes. The range is modular — modules parallel without a practical limit, so the same platform covers a single wall-mounted unit and installations reaching 3000 A and beyond.
Q4: Can AHF be installed outdoors or in harsh environments?
Yes. Alongside IP20 and IP30/IP40 enclosures for indoor switchrooms, the range includes IP65 models for outdoor and dusty sites, plus anti-pollution and fanless low-noise versions where dust, corrosion or acoustic noise is the constraint.
