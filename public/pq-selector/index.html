How it works

Answer a few questions about your voltage, load type, power factor, and harmonic condition. The selector estimates harmonic current for AHF sizing, reactive power demand for SVG sizing, then recommends the nearest CHITEK product configuration.

Sizing logic

What the selector calculates

  • AHF sizing from estimated harmonic current and THDi severity
  • SVG sizing from the power factor correction gap in kvar
  • Hybrid recommendation when harmonic and reactive issues are both significant
  • Nearest CHITEK module from the AHF selection matrix, with parallel modules above 200 A
